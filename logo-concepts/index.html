Lab Atlas · identity exploration

Fifty marks, no words.

Each concept is a single symbol for Lab Atlas, the directory that shows whether a research lab works at the bench, at a computer or both. Every mark is built on a 64-unit grid in one ink colour and one gold accent, with two softer tones of the ink where a mark needs more than one shade. Nothing is tied to one university.

How they were made

Five methods from the workspace's brand-kit guidance: monogram plus meaning, product action, metaphor fusion, negative space and construction geometry. No mark uses more than two ideas at once.

Small-size check

The two chips on each card show the mark at 32 and 16 pixels, the sizes for an app icon and a browser tab. A mark that falls apart at 16 needs a simpler favicon version.

The shortlist

The seven gold-tagged marks carry the most meaning in the fewest strokes. Round-Bottom Globe and Prism A each read as both lab and atlas. Wet ∩ Dry and Bubbles to Pixels draw the product's own categories.
