Emblems from an atlas plate

These 24 emblems are drawn the way old atlas plates and academic seals were: hatching, stipple, graduated rims, laurels and stars. Each is shown in two sample palettes. Fine engraving disappears at icon size, so every emblem also has a simplified small version, shown at 32 and 16 pixels on each card.

Two palettes

Navy & gold keeps the product's navy and gold, with cream for highlights. Earth uses a sepia ink on parchment, with ochre, sage and burnt sienna. Neither uses the old blue, pink and red.

Full emblem and small mark

The full emblem is for the homepage, social cards, stickers and print. The small mark keeps only the emblem's central device, for favicons and app icons.

Strongest candidates

Armillary Flask, Globe on an A, Celestial Flask and Meridian A each read as lab and atlas at once. Globe Seal and Wax Seal are the most seal-like. Astrolabe and Portolan Rose lean furthest toward the old atlas.
