Lab Atlas · round eight

Bold, flat, one glyph

Fifteen marks drawn to the conventions of the Y Combinator Fall 2026 batch. I looked at all 127 of its logos, and these follow what the strongest ones share. Each is shown on black, white and navy, with a switch for the accent colour.

One heavy glyph

Nearly every mark is a single flat symbol on a black or white square, with no gradients and no illustration. The few detailed marks in the batch are the weakest at small size.

Letters cut from geometry

K, M, Σ, λ, G and H, and several A's: Silica, Maritime, Vexo, Asakana. Ageospatial already uses an A with a globe, so these avoid that.

One accent at most

Mantle, Roma, Spine and Hesper add a single orange to an otherwise monochrome mark. The rest use no colour at all.

Pixels and stencils

Bitmap marks (Cyclon, Grep, Kailash) and stencil cuts (Simantic, isoquant) are a visible trend. So is heavy rounded geometry (Lyon, Capveon).

Accent

01  Pole A

Pole A at full weight: two meridians meet in a sharp point at the north pole, and the equator is a single accent bar. Nothing else.

02  Knockout Globe

A solid sphere with Pole A cut clean through it as negative space. It's a stencil, in the way of Simantic and isoquant in the batch.

03  Bitmap Globe

A globe drawn in 9-unit pixels with the A left out of the grid as negative space, and two accent pixels at the pole. It belongs to the batch's pixel-mark trend (Cyclon, Grep, Kailash) and stays sharp at 16 px.

04  North Star A

A four-point compass star whose north point is cut with a crossbar notch, so the top of the star is an A. Navigation and the initial in one shape.

05  LA Blocks

L and A as two heavy geometric blocks: a solid L and a solid triangle with its counter cut. They share a baseline and the A has an accent square, much as Roma and Mantle use one bright shape.

06  One Stroke

L into A in one heavy, unbroken stroke with square ends, like Keres or Hesper. The A's crossbar is the only accent.

07  Titan

Atlas reduced to two shapes: a sphere carried on an arch of shoulders and raised arms. This is the batch's heavy rounded geometry, in the manner of Capveon and Lyon. It's the titan without the illustration.

08  Pin A

A map pin whose head is cut with a solid A. It marks where a lab is, and the cut-out A doubles as the pin's eye.

09  Graticule Cell

A heavy 3×3 piece of the globe's grid, curved like a sphere, with one cell filled: the lab you were looking for.

10  Compass

A heavy compass ring with a north wedge in the accent colour and a centre pin. It's the compass markings you liked, at app-icon weight.

11  Lens

A magnifier whose lens is a globe: search across the atlas. The meridians inside the lens form a small A.

12  Folded Map

A folded map seen edge-on: three panels, with the middle fold rising to an accent peak. The whole shape can read as an M, so it's the weakest A in the set.

13  Latitude Bars

A sphere made of eight latitude bars, with a solid accent A standing through the stack. Stripes show up in the batch too (Hue, Ignition Benefits).

14  Pole Star

A solid globe with an accent four-point star at its north pole, sitting on the rim. It's the simplest globe in the set, and the star does all the work.

15  Meridian Point

The quietest version: one thin curved A of two meridians and an accent dot at the pole, in the restraint of Silica and Maritime.
