## Gold Niche (once selected in Phase 2)

- **Person:** Directors of undergraduate research offices at research universities with an academic medical center
- **Problem:** Students can't see which of thousands of investigators run labs that fit and take undergraduates, so they cold-email blindly, and posting tools stall because faculty won't volunteer
- **Promise:** More students in the right labs, zero faculty effort

## Competitive Intelligence (once completed in Phase 3)

- **Competitors Profiled:** 7 (ForagerOne, Handshake, homegrown platforms incl. PairMe, research-profile systems, AI assistants, status quo; "Connectagen" unverified)
- **Market Concentration:** Fragmented, with one specialist leader (ForagerOne, 200+ institutions)
- **Key Opportunity:** Be the discovery layer for large biomedical research enterprises, complementing the posting tool offices already own, and own the placement data nobody records

## Positioning (once completed in Phase 4)

- **Positioning Statement:** For directors of undergraduate research at research universities with medical centers who need more students in the right labs without adding work for faculty, Lab Atlas is a lab discovery platform that maps every investigator on campus from publications, grants and directories. Unlike opportunity-posting platforms that only show faculty who opt in, Lab Atlas covers every lab from the record and links to the posting tool you already use.
- **Market Category:** Lab discovery platform (subcategory)

## Core Offer (once built in Phase 5)

- **Product:** Hosted campus atlas (SSO) with wet-to-dry setting, evidence, graph; faculty one-click verify; posting-tool deep links; placement record + semester impact report; HECVAT/ACR pack; advisor training; monthly refresh
- **Price:** $15,000/yr list for a ≤3,000-investigator campus ($30,000 for ≤10,000); founding partners 40-50% off on 2-year terms
- **Name:** "Every Lab in 45 Days -- Founding Partner Program"
