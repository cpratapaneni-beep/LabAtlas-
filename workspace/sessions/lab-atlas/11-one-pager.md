> Generated: 2026-09-30 | Session: `lab-atlas` | Status: Draft (founder inputs pending)

## The Niche
- **Person:** Directors of undergraduate research offices at research universities with an academic medical center (Emory's Pathways Center first)
- **Problem:** Students can't see which of thousands of investigators run labs that fit and take undergraduates, so they cold-email blindly, and posting tools stall because faculty won't volunteer
- **Promise:** More students in the right labs, zero faculty effort
- **Scores:** Taki: Like G / Help G / Pay Y. Hormozi: Painful Y / Purchasing power G / Targetable G / Growing Y (**4/7 greens**; Silver: graduate rotations; Bronze: research development + landscape reports)

## Competitive Landscape
- **Competitors profiled:** 7 (ForagerOne, Handshake, homegrown incl. PairMe, research-profile systems, AI assistants, status quo; "Connectagen" unverified)
- **Market concentration:** fragmented, with one specialist leader (ForagerOne, 200+ institutions, **already at Emory's Pathways Center**)
- **Key opportunity:** be the discovery layer for large biomedical research enterprises, complementing the posting tool offices already own, and own the placement data nobody records
- **Top threat:** budget freezes, plus a discovery layer the incumbent or an in-house LLM build could copy within ~12 months

## Market Positioning
- **Position:** For directors of undergraduate research at research universities with medical centers who need more students in the right labs without adding work for faculty, Lab Atlas is a lab discovery platform that maps every investigator on campus from publications, grants and directories. Unlike posting platforms that only show faculty who opt in, it covers every lab and links to the posting tool you already use.
- **Onliness:** Lab Atlas is the only lab discovery platform that maps every investigator on campus, and whether each lab works at the bench or the keyboard, without asking faculty to do anything.
- **Category:** lab discovery platform (subcategory)
- **Value themes:** see every lab, know the lab before you write, fair access you can measure (after the pilot)

- **Price:** $15,000/yr list (≤3,000 investigators), $30,000/yr (≤10,000); founding partners 40-50% off on 2-year terms; $2,500 semester pilot credited to year one
