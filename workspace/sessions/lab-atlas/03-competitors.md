## Executive Summary
1. **ForagerOne** is the specialist leader (200+ institutions, bootstrapped, founded by JHU undergrads from a scraped Google Sheet, the same origin as Lab Atlas). It is **already paid for at Emory** by the Pathways Center's Undergraduate Research Programs. [Data]
2. Everything else is a good-enough substitute: Handshake postings, homegrown tools (PairMe serves GT/Emory BME), research-profile systems, AI assistants, and the real incumbent, cold email. [Data]
3. **The opening:** evidence-rich discovery that needs **zero faculty effort**, strongest in medical-center universities (87% of Emory's 7,078 investigators are School of Medicine; ~11% are predicted wet-bench). Posting platforms under voluntary faculty use showed no participation gain (ASEE 2026). [Data]
4. **The risk:** budget freezes plus a copyable discovery layer. The durable moat has to be proprietary **placement data** and institution relationships, not the website. [Opinion]
5. **Market:** fragmented around one specialist; a serviceable US pool of **≈$12M/yr** [Estimate]. Good for a focused company, but not venture-scale without expansion.

## Competitive Matrix
Lab Atlas v85 is **Strong** on coverage without faculty effort, evidence depth, method classification (unique), plain-language matching, the collaboration graph and provenance. It is **Missing** on postings/applications, analytics, enterprise readiness (SSO/HECVAT/VPAT) and multi-institution onboarding. **Gaps no one fills:** method classification, verified "took undergrads recently" data, equitable outreach coaching, outcome analytics tied to discovery.

## Key Opportunity
**Be the discovery layer for large biomedical research enterprises, sold as a complement to the workflow tool the office already owns, and own the placement data nobody records.**
Evidence: Emory's investigator base is 87% School of Medicine, with ~11% wet-bench [Data]; voluntary posting didn't move participation (ASEE 2026) [Data]; ForagerOne already sits at Emory and 200+ schools [Data]; no competitor records verified undergraduate placements [Data -- matrix].

## Strategic Risk
**Budget freezes plus a copyable feature set.** Emory froze hiring and cut operating spend in 2025; cuts continued nationally into 2026 [Data]. LLMs make evidence-based profiles cheap for ForagerOne or an in-house team to replicate within ~12 months [Opinion]. Mitigation: sub-threshold pricing, placement-data moat, partnership or licensing talks with ForagerOne.
