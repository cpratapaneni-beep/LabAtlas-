> **Adaptation note:** `/sk-leads` comes from high-velocity B2C sales (double-dialing, 5-minute callbacks; its "391%" and "78%" stats come from that world). Double-dialing a university administrator backfires. The pillars below keep the intent (be available, fast, personal, persistent) and translate it into enterprise higher-ed behavior.

## Step 1: Four Pillars of Lead Nurture

| Pillar | Diagnostic (today) | Score | Target behavior |
|--------|--------------------|:---:|-----------------|
| **Availability** | Student founders with class schedules; no booking link; no async demo | **4/10** | Self-scheduling link with 20-minute "preview walkthrough" slots across 4 weekdays; a recorded Loom walkthrough of *their* preview for people who can't meet |
| **Speed** | No inbound process; preview-build time unknown | **5/10** | Reply to any inbound within 1 business day; recap within 24h of every call; **preview atlas within 10 business days** |
| **Personalization** | Huge latent strength: every demo can be *their own campus* | **7/10** | Every touch references their institution's data (school mix, bench share, top clusters); proof matched to their type (medical-center R1 vs R2) |
| **Volume** | No CRM, no sequence, single-threaded (Pathways only) | **3/10** | 7-8 touches over ~6 weeks; **multi-thread** each account (UR director + associate dean + pre-health advising + grad program director); BAMFAM on every call |
| **Total** | | **19/40** | |

**Biggest leaks (below 6): Volume (3), Availability (4), Speed (5).** Every point here converts directly into more meetings. The cheapest fix: a free CRM (even a shared spreadsheet), a booking link and a written sequence (below).

### 4) LinkedIn Sales Navigator (precision targeting)
- **Titles:** Director / Assistant or Associate Dean of Undergraduate Research; Director, Office of Undergraduate Research; Director of Research Experiences; Director of Experiential Learning; Pre-Health Advising Director; Director of Graduate Studies (biomedical umbrella programs).
- **Institutions:** R1/R2 with a medical school. Start with the **Southeast** (GA, NC, SC, TN, AL, FL, VA), then national.

### 5) Partnerships (a channel most startups miss)
- **ForagerOne:** after the Emory LOI, propose a deep-link integration and a data partnership (method tags and evidence feeding their profiles). Outcomes range from co-selling to licensing to acquisition, all better than a head-on fight with 200+ institutions of distribution.
- **Research-profile system owners** at customer sites (libraries, research offices): ingest their data to cut onboarding cost.

### 6) Cold outreach (last resort)
- A list of ~60 medical-center R1/R2 universities, 3-touch email over 3 weeks, offering a free preview atlas. Volume target: 15/week once the preview pipeline takes ≤2 days per campus.

## Step 3: Nurture Sequence (enterprise version)

| When | Channel | Action |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Day 0** (same day as call) | Email | Thank-you + 3-bullet recap of *their* numbers + date the preview atlas will arrive |
| **Day 3-10** | Email + Loom | Deliver the **preview atlas** + 2-page landscape snapshot, with a 3-minute personal walkthrough video |

| **Day 30** | Email + call | Proposal: founding-partner terms, **deadline Mar 31, 2027**; semester-pilot downsell |

| **Jan-Feb** | Email | Budget-season reminder with ready-to-paste budget request language |

## Step 4: Show-Rate Optimization Checklist (for demos and preview walkthroughs)
- [ ] Walkthrough scheduled within 10 business days of the discovery call (72h is unrealistic for a preview build; set the expectation up front)
- [ ] Self-scheduling link + founder-initiated invites + reply-to-book (all three modes)
- [ ] 20-minute slots on at least 4 weekdays (block them around class schedules)
- [ ] Automated reminders: 24h and 1h before (skip the 12h/3h B2C cadence for administrators)
- [ ] Personal reminder the morning of, with one teaser insight from their preview
- [ ] Incentive to show: the landscape snapshot is presented live (sent afterwards)
- [ ] Personalized proof: a peer-type institution's result (Emory pilot, once available)
- [ ] BAMFAM: book the advisor-team session before ending the walkthrough
- [ ] Qualifying form before the walkthrough: tools owned, renewal date, budget owner, directory export available?
- [ ] Always use the prospect's time zone

## Red Flags
- **Volume 3/10 and Emory-only network:** the pipeline depends on one champion's introductions. If those don't materialize by November, the Dec 18 target of 10 calls fails.

## Yellow Flags
- Student availability collides with academic calendars (finals in December, spring break in March, exactly when budget conversations happen). Plan coverage.
- Emory data in public content needs Pathways Center or Communications sign-off.
