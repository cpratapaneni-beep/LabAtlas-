# 1-Minute Elevator Pitch: Lab Atlas
*Skill: sk-pitch | Generated: 2026-09-30 | ~150 words each*

## Formal (accelerator panel / investor event)
> Lab Atlas maps every research lab at a university: what each studies, whether it works at the bench or at a keyboard, and who it works with. We build it from publications, grants and directories, with zero work from faculty.
>
> Why it matters: at Emory, 7,078 investigators are listed, but our model puts only about 810 of them on the bench side. Students who want lab work cold-email blindly, and the students who get answers are the ones who already know how the system works.
>
> Posting platforms can't fix that, because most faculty never post. We map everyone from the record and link to the posting tool the university already owns.
>
> We built the full Emory atlas and have the Pathways Center's support. Now we're turning it into a product universities license. **[The Ask: see pitch-2min]**

## Casual (networking)
> You know how students find research labs? They cold-email professors off department pages and hope. We built a map of every lab at Emory, 7,078 investigators, that shows what each lab studies and whether it's bench or computer work, straight from their papers and grants. No faculty data entry. Emory's Pathways Center is backing us, and we're turning it into something any research university can license. Do you know anyone who runs undergraduate research at a university with a medical school?

## Delivery notes
- Pause after "only about 810 of them." That's the moment the problem becomes concrete.
- Never say "AI-powered"; say "built from the record".
- Don't use Emory figures publicly until the Pathways Center and Emory Communications approve.
