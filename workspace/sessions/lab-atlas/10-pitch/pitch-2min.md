# 2-Minute Pitch Script: Lab Atlas
*Skill: sk-pitch | Generated: 2026-09-30 | ~300 words, word-for-word*

> **[What we do]** Lab Atlas maps every research lab at a university: what each one studies, whether it works at the bench or at a keyboard, and who it works with. We build it from publications, grants and directories, so faculty don't lift a finger.
>
> **[Insight]** Here's what we learned building it for Emory. The directory lists 7,078 investigators. Our classifier (91.6% accurate on a held-out test) places only about 810 of them on the bench side. So a pre-med who wants lab work is looking for a few hundred needles in a 7,000-name haystack. Today she finds them by cold email, and research shows faculty answer some names more than others.
>
> **[Why existing tools don't fix it]** Universities already buy posting platforms like ForagerOne. But posting only works when faculty post, and at one university voluntary posting produced no increase in participation at all. We don't compete with those tools. We map every lab from the record and link each profile to the posting tool the school already pays for.
>
> **[Traction]** We've built the complete Emory atlas, now on version 85, with the support of Emory's Pathways Center. Next: a Spring 2027 pilot with measured outcomes (placements, reply rates, equity gaps).
>
> **[Business model]** Universities license it per campus: $15,000 a year for a typical campus, $30,000 for a large medical-center university. Founding partners get half off in exchange for outcome data.
>
> **[Market]** There are 542 research-active US universities. At those prices that's roughly a $12 million-a-year core market, and more with graduate and research-development modules.
>
> **[Team]** [Founder 1 -- one specific accomplishment]. [Founder 2 -- one specific accomplishment].
>
> **[The Ask]** We're asking for **[a seat in the Techstars Emory Founder Catalyst 2027 cohort / $150K in non-dilutive and pre-seed funding]** to reach five paying universities and a 14-day onboarding by June 2027. We'd also love introductions to anyone who runs undergraduate research at a university with a medical school.

## Delivery notes
- **Pace:** ~150 words per minute. Slow down on the 810 / 7,078 contrast.
- **Emphasis:** "faculty don't lift a finger", "we don't compete with those tools".
- **Invite conversation** after the insight. If a judge asks "why not ForagerOne?", you've won their attention.
