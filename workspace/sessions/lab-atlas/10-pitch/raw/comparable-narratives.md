# Raw: Comparable Narratives (Wave 1)
*Skill: sk-pitch | Research date: 2026-09-30*

- **ForagerOne (the closest comparable).** JHU undergrads, a Google Sheet of ~100-200 scraped life-science faculty, grassroots student adoption, then institutional contracts, a pandemic-era second product (Symposium), MassChallenge, a $30K Bisciotti Prize, and **200+ institutions without venture capital** (as of 2021-2024). Lesson for the narrative: *student-founded, revenue-first, institution-by-institution* is a proven path in this exact market. [Data]
- **PairMe (Georgia Tech).** One student saw mentors were "largely passive when seeking mentees" and built a posting board adopted campus-wide. Lesson: a campus will adopt a student tool, but growth stops at the campus boundary without a company. [Data]
- **Notre Dame platform (ASEE 2026).** Mandated use raised research-for-credit participation +57.5% across student groups; voluntary use didn't. Lesson for the insight: *coverage without faculty effort* is the lever when mandates aren't possible. [Data]
