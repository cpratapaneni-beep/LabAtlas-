# Raw: Competitive Framing (Wave 2)
*Skill: sk-pitch | Research date: 2026-09-30*

- **Frame:** "ForagerOne and Handshake run the *transaction* (post, apply, message). Lab Atlas does the *discovery*: which of 7,000 investigators a student should write to, and whether the lab works at the bench or the keyboard. We link to them; we don't replace them."
- **Why investors will push:** "ForagerOne is at Emory already. Why won't they just add this?" Answer: they might. So the moat is (1) placement data nobody records, (2) a pipeline that onboards a campus in 14 days, and (3) an open partnership posture (licensing or acquisition are good outcomes).
- **Avoid:** "no competition", "ForagerOne killer", "AI-powered".
