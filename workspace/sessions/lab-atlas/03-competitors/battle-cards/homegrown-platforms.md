# Battle Card: Homegrown Platforms (PairMe, Notre Dame's platform, UConnect, etc.)
*Last updated: 2026-09-30 | Threat: MEDIUM (category) / LOW-MEDIUM (PairMe locally)*

## At a Glance
- **What they do:** Campus-built posting/matching tools. **PairMe** (Georgia Tech, created by BME student Amy Liu): mentors post openings, students apply with GT login, now all GT majors plus the joint GT/Emory BME department. [Data]
- **Founded:** various | **Funding:** internal | **Team:** students or staff
- **Price:** free to users; hidden staff cost
- **Best for:** a single campus with a mandate (Notre Dame Engineering mandated use: +57.5% research-for-credit participation). [Data]

## Their Strengths (be honest)
- Local trust, free, and can be **mandated** by a dean. The ASEE data shows mandates drive results. [Data]
- Fit to local processes (research credit, forms).

## Their Weaknesses (your openings)
- Maintenance dies when the student builder graduates or the staffer leaves. [Opinion]
- Posting-only coverage (same as Handshake).
- No evidence layer (publications, grants, methods).

## How to Win Against Them
- **When they say "We'll build our own with AI,"** respond: "You can build the website. The hard part is the pipeline: merging directories, matching publications to the right person despite namesakes, classifying methods at 91.6% held-out accuracy, and refreshing monthly. That's what we maintain so your staff don't."
- **Lead with:** total cost of ownership and continuity.

## When They Win Over You
- A dean will mandate a local tool and staff it. Tiny institutions where everyone knows everyone.

## Their Customers' Top Complaint
"Mentors were largely passive when seeking mentees" (PairMe's origin story): posting tools inherit faculty passivity. [Data]

## Key Vulnerability
Continuity and coverage.

## Churn Signals
- Builder graduation; platform goes stale. [Opinion]

## Watch For
- PairMe expanding into Emory beyond BME; LLM-built internal tools at well-resourced universities. [Opinion]
