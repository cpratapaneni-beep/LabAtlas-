# Battle Card: ForagerOne
*Last updated: 2026-09-30 | Threat: HIGH*

## At a Glance
- **What they do:** University-wide marketplace where faculty profiles, research postings, applications and student-faculty messages live in one place; plus Symposium for research events.
- **Founded:** ~2017-18 (JHU students) | **Funding:** bootstrapped as of 2021 + $30K Bisciotti Prize (2020) | **Team:** DATA GAP
- **Price:** not public (est. $8-40K/yr per institution) | **Model:** institution license; free to students and faculty
- **Best for:** UR offices that want one managed workflow (post, apply, message, report) across all disciplines, humanities included.
- **At Emory:** run by Emory College URP inside the **Pathways Center**, i.e. your champion's office.

## Their Strengths (be honest)
- **Distribution and trust:** 200+ institutions (2024), with steady 2023-25 launches (Hawaiʻi, Baylor, Wayne State, UMD). [Data]
- **Full workflow:** postings, applications, messenger routed to email, SSO, admin analytics. Lab Atlas has none of these today. [Data]
- **All disciplines:** covers "research and creative activity" beyond STEM. [Data]
- **Second product (Symposium)** deepens the account relationship. [Data]
- **Faculty profiles auto-created** from university web pages or profile systems. [Data]

## Their Weaknesses (your openings)
- **Participation-dependent freshness:** the "Accepting Students" toggle and postings only work if faculty act. New faculty lack auto-created profiles until onboarded (Mizzou). [Data]
- **Posting-first model:** ASEE 2026 found voluntary faculty use of a posting platform produced no participation gain. [Data -- a similar platform, not ForagerOne itself]
- **No evidence of method classification, grant signals or collaboration graphs** in public materials. [Data gap -- confirm in a demo]

## How to Win Against Them
- **When they say "We already have ForagerOne,"** respond: "Keep it. Lab Atlas tells students *which* of your 7,000 investigators to message, and every profile links straight to that person's ForagerOne page. You get more, better-targeted messages through the tool you already pay for."
- **When they say "ForagerOne already has faculty profiles,"** respond: "How many faculty have claimed theirs and flipped 'accepting'? Lab Atlas covers every investigator from publications, grants and directories, with no faculty effort, and shows whether each lab works at the bench or at a keyboard."
- **When they say "We can't pay for two tools,"** respond: "Then start with a one-semester, one-cohort pilot priced under your approval threshold, measured on placements. If outcomes don't move, don't renew."
- **Lead with:** the wet-to-dry spectrum and "every lab, no faculty effort" on *their own campus* (bring a preview).

## When They Win Over You
- Institutions that need **postings, applications and messaging workflows** now (Lab Atlas has none).
- **Humanities and creative-activity** matching (Lab Atlas is STEM/biomedical-centric).
- Buyers who need **SSO, HECVAT, VPAT and an 8-year vendor track record** this procurement cycle.
- Offices that also want **symposium tooling** from the same vendor.

## Their Customers' Top Complaint
No public reviews exist. Closest proxy: the Mizzou FAQ notes new faculty "will not have an auto created file" and must be onboarded, i.e. coverage lags. [Data] **Implication:** ask customers about faculty participation rates; that is the pressure point.

## Key Vulnerability
Its value is capped by faculty behavior. Lab Atlas's is not. Prove it with Emory's own ForagerOne analytics (claim rate, share accepting) next to Lab Atlas coverage (7,078 investigators; wet/dry calls on 76.6% of records). [Data -- 5,424 of 7,078 are classified]

## Churn Signals
- Renewal plus low faculty participation plus budget freeze -- **frequency: unknown (no public data)**.
- Offices consolidating onto Handshake to save money -- **occasional** [Opinion].

## Watch For
- **AI-generated profile enrichment** (topic tags, method inference) would erase much of Lab Atlas's discovery edge. [Opinion]
- **Partnership or acquisition appetite:** a bootstrapped leader may prefer licensing a proven classifier and pipeline to building one. Keep that door open.
