# Pricing Landscape: Lab Atlas
*Skill: sk-competitors | Generated: 2026-09-30*

## Market Pricing Overview
- **Dominant value metric:** per-institution annual license (flat or tiered by institution size); students never pay. [Data + Inference]
- **Price range (comparable office tools):** $0 (homegrown, open source, Handshake add-on) to ~$25K/yr (InfoReady Gold, SFA State FY24-25) [Data], to six figures for enterprise research-information systems [Knowledge-based]. ForagerOne is estimated at **$8K-$40K/yr** [Estimate, Low confidence].
- **Median point for a dedicated UR-office tool:** ~$15-25K/yr. [Estimate]
- **Free options:** 3 of 7 alternatives are free or already paid for (status quo, homegrown, Handshake for research postings).

## Value Metric Analysis
| Competitor | Value Metric | Why it works / doesn't | Scaling impact |
|-----------|-------------|------------------------|----------------|
| ForagerOne | Institution license (+ Symposium packages) | Simple for offices; invisible to students | Revenue grows with the number of institutions, not usage |
| InfoReady | Institution package (Gold, etc.) | Predictable budget line | Upsell via packages |
| Handshake | Career-center license | Research piggybacks at zero marginal cost | Hard to beat on price |
| Profile systems | Institution license by size/modules | Tied to research-office budgets | High price, high switching cost |
| **Lab Atlas (proposed)** | **Institution license tiered by research-active investigators covered** | Aligns price with pipeline cost and navigation pain (bigger enterprise = more value) | Natural expansion as more schools are added |

## Pricing Whitespace
1. **Below departmental approval thresholds.** A sub-$10-15K add-on can often be approved by a dean or director without an RFP (RFPs are common above ~$50K; thresholds vary). [Data -- Tier 3 + Opinion]
2. **Module pricing across offices.** One atlas, many budgets (undergrad research, graduate programs, research development, medical education). Nobody prices this way. [Opinion]
3. **Outcome-linked founding pricing.** Discount in exchange for published outcomes (a case study plus a co-authored NCUR/CUR presentation). [Opinion]
4. **Grant-fundable line item.** Priced and documented so program PIs can put it in training-grant or HHMI-style budgets. [Opinion]

## Recommendations
- **If competing on price:** don't. Handshake and homegrown tools are free; a race to the bottom loses.
- **If competing on value:** tier by investigators covered. **List** ~$6K (program/department, ≤500 PIs), ~$15K (≤3,000), ~$30K (≤10,000, typical medical-center university), custom above that. Onboarding fee $2.5-5K, waived on 2-year terms.
- **If competing on model:** one atlas, many office modules (undergrad, graduate, research development), plus a **founding-partner** price (≈40-50% off list for the first 5 institutions) in exchange for outcomes data, a case study and a reference call.

## Red Flags
- **ForagerOne's price is unknown.** Every recommendation here is anchored to proxies. Get Emory's number before quoting anyone.

## Yellow Flags
- Institution counts overstate the reachable market: many RCUs and R2s lack medical centers, so Lab Atlas's advantage is smaller there.
- Budget freezes may push realistic ACVs toward the low end of each tier for 2026-27.
