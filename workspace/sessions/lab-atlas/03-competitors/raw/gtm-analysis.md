# Raw: Go-to-Market Analysis (Wave 3 / C1)
*Skill: sk-competitors | Research date: 2026-09-30*

## ForagerOne
- **Motion:** sales-led institutional deals with Offices of Undergraduate Research, then a campus launch (news story + help pages + faculty demo workshops). [Data]
- **Land product:** Symposium (virtual/hybrid research symposia, a universal need for UR offices). Around 50 institutions used it in 2020, a second foothold with the same buyer. [Data]
- **Channels:** NCUR/CUR presence (2024 NCUR listing); campus pages act as de facto SEO and reference assets; word of mouth among UR directors. [Data -> Inference]
- **Expansion pattern:** grad students, postdocs, medical/vet students and research staff can access with credentials; the rollout prioritizes undergrads and faculty (Mizzou). [Data]

## Handshake
- Career-center-led; research is a secondary use case promoted by UR offices through how-to pages. [Data]

## PairMe
- Student-built, department-sponsored, then campus-wide. No external sales. [Data]
