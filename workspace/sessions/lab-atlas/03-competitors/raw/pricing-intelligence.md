## Procurement thresholds (why price points matter)
- Higher-ed purchasing is committee-driven, and RFPs are common above ~$50K; department chairs or deans can often approve smaller software contracts. [Data -- vendor blog, Tier 3; thresholds vary by university]
- Most universities run July-June fiscal years, with departmental budget requests due Jan-Feb. [Data -- Tier 3]
- Typical higher-ed sales cycle: 6-18 months; decentralized departmental purchases 3-9 months. [Data -- Tier 3]
