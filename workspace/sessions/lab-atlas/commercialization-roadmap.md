> Session: `lab-atlas` | Generated: 2026-09-30 | Synthesis of StartupKit phases 1-11

---

## The short answer

1. **You're not at "mass" yet. You're at "first contract."** Nothing scales until four gates are passed: **(1) the right to sell, (2) a contracted design partner, (3) proof the pipeline works at a second university, (4) procurement readiness.** Mass commercialization is what happens *after* those four gates, roughly FY2028 onward.
2. **Your champion already pays your main competitor.** Emory College's Undergraduate Research Programs, inside the Pathways Center, runs **ForagerOne** (200+ institutions). Position Lab Atlas as the **discovery layer that makes ForagerOne pay off** (every profile links to it), not as a replacement. Then turn "support" into a **signed design-partner LOI with metrics** by Nov 30.
3. **Clear the IP and the name before talking to any other university.** Under Emory Policy 7.6, students own IP unless Emory funding, substantial Emory support or an external funding agreement was involved. Get OTT's determination in writing. Drop "Emory" from the product name.
4. **Prove repeatability.** Build a second university's atlas from open data in **≤14 days and ≤40 hours**. If you can't, "mass" is a pipeline problem, not a sales problem.
5. **Sell 5 founding partners for FY2028** (contracts starting July 1, 2027). Start conversations **before Jan-Feb 2027**, when budget requests are due. Price below approval thresholds ($8-15K founding; $15-30K list) and fund year one **non-dilutively**.

## The four gates (in order)

| Gate | What "done" looks like | Target date | Why it blocks everything |
|------|------------------------|:---:|--------------------------|
| **1. Right to sell** | Written OTT determination (student-owned, released or licensed); a legal entity with founder IP assignment; a new name with a trademark knockout search; data-source rights documented | **Nov 15, 2026** | Universities ask for IP warranties in every contract. Selling Emory-owned IP or using Emory's name without approval is listed as policy noncompliance. |
| **2. Contracted design partner** | Emory LOI: Spring 2027 pilot, agreed metrics, data access, reference rights, peer introductions | **Nov 30, 2026** | "Support" doesn't show up in a procurement file. A signed document does. |
| **3. Repeatability** | "Atlas #2" preview for a non-Emory medical-center university from open data in ≤14 days / ≤40 hrs, ≥90% coverage | **Jan 15, 2027** | Mass = onboarding cost × number of campuses. Unknown cost = unknown business. |
| **4. Procurement readiness** | Hosted multi-tenant app; SAML SSO; WCAG 2.1 AA + accessibility conformance report (VPAT); HECVAT 4; privacy policy and DPA; faculty correct/opt-out; insurance certificates | **Apr 30, 2027** | Public universities must meet ADA Title II web accessibility by **Apr 26, 2027**, and HECVAT is the standard security gate (now with AI questions). |

## Biggest risks (and what to do about them)
1. **"Support" never becomes a contract.** Mitigation: a dated LOI ask with a $0 floor. If there's no LOI by December, pivot to graduate programs or to licensing through ForagerOne.
2. **IP ownership is unclear** (Pathways support, possible Emory data or funding). Mitigation: OTT first; license if needed. Emory keeps a royalty-free educational license either way.
3. **The incumbent copies the discovery layer.** Mitigation: placement-data moat, speed, partnership posture.
4. **Budget freezes.** Mitigation: sub-threshold pricing, semester pilots credited to year one, grant-fundable framing.
5. **Faculty backlash** (wrong labels, exposed emails, spammy outreach). Mitigation: SSO-gated emails, one-click correction and opt-out, send limits, faculty advisors.
6. **Founder continuity.** Mitigation: entity, vesting, advisors, data-export terms. Decide who stays full-time after graduation.
