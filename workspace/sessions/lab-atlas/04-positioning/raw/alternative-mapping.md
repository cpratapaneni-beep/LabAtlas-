# Raw: Alternative Mapping (Wave 1 / A1, JTBD lens)
*Skill: sk-positioning | Research date: 2026-09-30 | Builds on 03-competitors/raw/*

| Alternative | Job the office or student hires it for | Where it falls short | Switching trigger |
|-------------|------------------------------------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Cold email + faculty pages + advisor lists | "Help me find *someone* who'll say yes" | Inequitable; untargeted; invisible labs; advisor time | Equity mandate; participation KPI; a bad semester of placements |
| ForagerOne | "Run our research-opportunity marketplace" | Coverage and freshness bounded by faculty opt-in; no method or evidence layer found | Renewal plus low faculty participation; budget review |
| Handshake postings | "Put paid RA jobs in front of students" | Only posted roles | Office wants to show unposted labs |
| Homegrown tools (PairMe, Notre Dame) | "Our own posting board, our rules" | Continuity; coverage | Builder graduates; staff leaves |
| Research-profile systems | "Find a faculty expert" | Not built for students | Student-success initiative needs a student view |
| AI assistants | "Give me a quick list of labs doing X" | No verified local truth | Hallucinated or stale answers erode trust |
| Doing nothing | "Students are adults; they'll manage" | Hidden curriculum persists | New leadership; accreditation or strategic plan emphasizing access |

**The full set Lab Atlas replaces or augments:** hand-curated advisor lab lists, department web crawling, blind cold email, and the discovery half of posting platforms.
