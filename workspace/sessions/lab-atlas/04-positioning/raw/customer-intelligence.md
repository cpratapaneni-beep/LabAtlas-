# Raw: Customer Intelligence (Wave 1 / A2)
*Skill: sk-positioning | Research date: 2026-09-30*

## Pain points with current alternatives (evidence)
- Informal, network-dependent access; a "hidden door". [ASEE 2026]
- Faculty passivity: "mentors were largely passive when seeking mentees". [PairMe origin]
- Competitive, time-consuming lab search with no official process. [Daily Bruin 2023]
- Response bias against women and minoritized names in faculty email replies (PhD context). [Milkman et al. audit]
- New faculty invisible until onboarded. [Mizzou ForagerOne FAQ]

## What "better" means (inferred)
- **Offices:** more students placed, more equitably, with less staff time and no extra faculty burden; numbers to show the provost.
- **Students:** know which labs fit (topic *and* method), which take undergrads, and how to write the first email.
- **Faculty:** fewer, better-targeted emails; control over whether they're "taking students".

## Best-fit customer profile (who gets value fastest)
1. Research university **with an academic medical center**: thousands of investigators, many in clinical departments.
2. Large **pre-health / life-science** undergraduate population looking for bench or clinical research. [Emory: pre-health advising sits in the Pathways Center -- Data]
3. The office is **measured on participation and equity** and has at least one data-minded champion.
4. Has a posting tool but **low faculty participation**, or no tool at all.
5. Budget authority at the director or associate-dean level (sub-threshold purchase possible).
