# Market Category Analysis: Lab Atlas
*Skill: sk-positioning | Generated: 2026-09-30*

## Candidate Categories
| # | Category | Type | Buyer expectations | Lab Atlas meets today? | Trade-off |
|---|----------|------|--------------------|:---:|-----------|
| 1 | Undergraduate research opportunity platform | Head-to-head | Postings, applications, messaging, SSO, analytics | No | Direct comparison with ForagerOne on its strengths |
| 2 | Research networking / expertise finder | Head-to-head | Faculty expertise search, reporting | Partly | Wrong buyer (research office), wrong user (faculty) |
| 3 | **Lab discovery platform** (for students, at research universities) | **Subcategory** | Complete coverage, rich evidence, fit signals, links to where you apply | **Yes** | Must explain why discovery deserves its own budget |
| 4 | Research access intelligence | Category creation | Equity and participation analytics | No | Expensive to educate; no data yet |
| 5 | Student research navigator (advising) | Subcategory | Case management, nudges | No | Drags in advising-CRM expectations |

## Recommendation: #3 "Lab discovery platform"
- **Why:** it's the frame where U1 (method spectrum) and U2/U3 (coverage with evidence) matter most, and it avoids the workflow comparison Lab Atlas would lose.
- **Category type:** subcategory, big fish in a small pond. Buyers already understand "undergraduate research tools"; "discovery" names the half of the job posting tools don't do.
- **Path to #4:** after the Emory pilot yields placement and equity data, add "access analytics" as a module and let the category grow into it.

## Implementation
- **Category label (use everywhere):** "lab discovery platform"
- **Tagline direction:** coverage plus fit ("See every lab. Write to the right one.")
- **Buyer expectation alignment:** say up front what Lab Atlas is *not* (not a posting or application system) and what it connects to (ForagerOne, Handshake).

## Red Flags
- If a buyer's budget line is literally "research opportunity platform", procurement may force a category-#1 comparison. Pre-empt it with the complement framing and sub-threshold pricing.

## Yellow Flags
- The category name is new to buyers; test "lab discovery" vs "research discovery" in the first 10 discovery calls.
