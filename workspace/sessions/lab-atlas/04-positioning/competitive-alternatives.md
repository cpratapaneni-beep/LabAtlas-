# Competitive Alternatives Map: Lab Atlas
*Skill: sk-positioning | Generated: 2026-09-30*

| Alternative | Type | Job hired for | Strengths | Shortcomings | Switching trigger | Lab Atlas's unique attributes vs. this alternative |
|-------------|------|---------------|-----------|--------------|-------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Cold email + pages + advisor lists | Status quo | Find anyone who'll say yes | Free; advisor judgment | Inequitable; untargeted; invisible labs | Equity mandate; participation KPI | U1 method spectrum, U2 coverage, U3 evidence |
| ForagerOne | Direct | Run the opportunity marketplace | Workflow, SSO, analytics, 200+ schools, Symposium | Faculty-opt-in coverage; no method layer found | Renewal plus low faculty participation | U1, U2 without opt-in, U3, U5 graph; **complements** via deep links |
| Handshake | Adjacent | Post paid RA jobs | Students already there; free to the office | Posted roles only | Need to show unposted labs | U2 coverage of never-posting labs |
| Homegrown (PairMe, etc.) | Adjacent | Local posting board | Free; mandate-able | Continuity; coverage | Builder turnover | Maintained pipeline; U1-U3 |
| Research-profile systems | Adjacent | Faculty expert finder | Publication depth | Not for students | Student-success initiative | Student-facing U1 + outreach; can ingest their data |
| AI assistants | Substitute | Quick idea list | Free, conversational | No verified local truth | Stale or wrong answers | Verified coverage; U6 honest confidence |
| Doing nothing | Status quo | -- | Zero cost | Hidden curriculum persists | Leadership change; strategic plan | Low-effort launch (atlas built from records) |

## Red Flags
- Against ForagerOne, Lab Atlas wins on **discovery** and loses on **workflow**. A buyer forced to choose one will usually pick workflow. So don't force the choice.

## Yellow Flags
- AI assistants improve monthly; re-test U4 (search) as a differentiator every quarter.
