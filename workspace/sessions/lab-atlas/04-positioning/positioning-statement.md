# Positioning Statements: Lab Atlas
*Skill: sk-positioning | Generated: 2026-09-30*

## Moore Positioning Statement
> **For** directors of undergraduate research at research universities with medical centers **who** need more students in the right labs without adding work for faculty, **Lab Atlas is a** lab discovery platform **that** maps every investigator on campus (what they study, whether they work at the bench or the keyboard, and who they work with) from publications, grants and directories. **Unlike** opportunity-posting platforms, which only show faculty who opt in, **Lab Atlas** covers every lab from the record and links straight to the posting tool you already use.

## Neumeier Onliness Statement
- **Basic:** Lab Atlas is the only lab discovery platform that maps every investigator on campus, and whether each lab works at the bench or the keyboard, without asking faculty to do anything.
- **Extended:** Lab Atlas is the only lab discovery platform that maps every investigator, with a measured wet-to-dry setting, publications, funding and collaborators, for undergraduate research offices at medical-center universities whose students must find fitting labs among thousands, so they write to fewer, better-matched labs, at a time when faculty won't maintain another profile.

## Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
> Most students find a research lab by cold-emailing professors they found on department pages. It wastes their time, floods faculty inboxes, and favors students who already know how the system works. Lab Atlas builds a complete map of every investigator on your campus from publications, grants and directories, with no faculty data entry. It tells students what each lab studies, whether it works at the bench or at a keyboard, and who it works with. Students write to fewer, better-matched labs. Your office sees the whole field. And every profile links to the ForagerOne or Handshake workflow you already run. At Emory it maps 7,078 investigators today.

## Freemium Positioning
Not recommended as a public freemium (institution-paid model; faculty data should stay behind SSO). Use a **free preview atlas** for prospects instead (see `05-offer.md` attraction offer).

## Red Flags
- Don't use statements containing "Emory" commercially until OTT, University Communications and General Counsel confirm what's allowed. The Emory Policy 7.6 brief lists misuse of the Emory name and unauthorized commercialization as noncompliance. Use "[University]" placeholders externally.

## Yellow Flags
- "Every investigator" is only true for the source records ingested (e.g., 112 Emory College STEM investigators). Qualify with "every investigator in [scope]" until coverage is complete.
