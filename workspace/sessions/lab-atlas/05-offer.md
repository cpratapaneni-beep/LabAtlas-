## Step 1: Six P's One-Pager

### Person (buyer avatar: "the UR Director")
- **Role:** Director or Assistant Dean of Undergraduate Research (or a Pathways-style center lead) at an R1/R2 **with an academic medical center**.
- **Profile:** often a PhD and former researcher; 1-5 staff; reports to an associate dean or vice provost; controls a small discretionary budget; renews 2-5 software tools a year. [Assumption]
- **Values and fears:** equity and access; faculty goodwill (won't burn it on a tool nobody uses); being blamed for a failed rollout; IT/security reviews; budget cuts.
- **Aspirations:** a visible jump in participation (especially first-gen), numbers for the provost, and a conference talk about what worked.
- **Hangouts:** CUR (UR Program Directors division), NCUR, CUR Dialogues, regional UR consortia, LinkedIn, CUR/NCUR listservs.
- **Reads:** CUR's *Scholarship and Practice of Undergraduate Research*, Inside Higher Ed, the Chronicle, EDUCAUSE Review (for the IT view). [Knowledge-based]
- **Secondary faces:** *students* (want to find fitting labs and not embarrass themselves) and *faculty* (want fewer, better emails and control over availability).

### Problem
- **Core pain:** "I can't see, or show students, which of our thousands of investigators run fitting labs that take undergrads. Faculty won't post or keep profiles current, and the students who most need help are the ones cold email filters out."
- **Why now:** budget reviews in a freeze; renewal of existing tools; participation and equity metrics in strategic plans; SACSCOC Quality Enhancement Plans at Southern schools often center experiential learning. [Opinion]
- **Cost of not solving:** hours of advisor time per student hand-building lab lists [Founder to measure]; missed placements; attrition of pre-health and STEM students who never get in a lab; inequity that shows up in outcomes data.

### Pain: **6/10**
Chronic and important rather than acute. In 6 months: another cohort misses the fall research window. In 2 years: participation flat, equity gap unchanged, office can't defend its budget.

### Promise
> **More students in the right labs, zero faculty effort.** (8 words)

### Plan (cliff-to-cliff bridge)
1. **Map (days 0-30):** we build your campus atlas from your directory export plus public records (publications, NIH/NSF awards). No faculty data entry.
2. **Verify (days 30-45):** faculty get one optional email: "Is this right? Taking undergrads this term? Yes / No / Later". One click, no login.
3. **Launch (days 45-60):** advisors and students sign in with campus SSO. Every profile links to your ForagerOne/Handshake page. Advisors get a 45-minute training.
4. **Measure (the semester):** placements and first contacts are captured in the placement record; you get a semester impact report.
5. **Refresh (ongoing):** monthly data refresh; new faculty appear automatically.

### Product (what the institution receives)
- Hosted campus atlas behind SSO (atlas view, plain-language search, directory, collaboration graph)
- Wet-to-dry setting with confidence, plus publications, NIH funding and collaborators on every profile
- Faculty one-click verify / correct / availability email
- Deep links to the institution's posting tool (ForagerOne, Handshake, PairMe)
- Placement record plus a **semester impact report** (use, contacts, placements, with privacy-safe equity breakdowns)
- Advisor training + student workshop kit (slides, 1-page "how to write to a lab" guide)
- Monthly data refresh

*Less is more:* no postings, applications or messaging. The office already owns those.

### Price
| Tier (investigators covered) | List (annual) | Founding partner (first 5 institutions, 2-year term) |
|---|---|---|
| Program / department (≤500) | $6,000 | $3,500 |
| Standard (≤3,000) | $15,000 | $8,000 |
| **Research university (≤10,000; typical medical-center university)** | **$30,000** | **$15,000** |
| System / >10,000 | Custom | Custom |
| Onboarding (one-time) | $2,500-5,000 | **Waived** on 2-year terms |

**StartupKit revenue math** (at the $15K mid-tier list price):
- Skill formula (new clients per month for one-time revenue): $100K/yr needs 100,000 / 15,000 / 12 = **0.56 new institutions/month**; $1M/yr needs **5.6/month**.
- SaaS reality (recurring): **$100K ARR = ~7 institutions; $1M ARR = ~67 institutions** at a $15K average. ForagerOne's 200+ shows 67 is reachable over years, not months. [Data + Estimate]

**Emory design-partner offer (the first deal):** a Spring 2027 pilot (one advising cohort plus one first-year research course) for **$0-5,000 or in-kind**, in exchange for (1) a signed letter of intent with success metrics, (2) access to baseline outcomes and Emory's ForagerOne analytics, (3) permission to reference Emory (pending Communications approval), (4) help getting a clear OTT determination, and (5) warm introductions to 5 peer UR directors. If IP is released to you, Emory keeps a royalty-free educational license under Policy 7.6, but hosting, refresh and support are still billable services. [Data + Opinion]

## Step 5: Enhance the Offer
- **Scarcity (real):** **5 founding-partner slots** for AY2027-28. Onboarding capacity for a student team is genuinely limited.
- **Urgency:** founding pricing is honored for agreements signed by **March 31, 2027** (budget requests for FY2028 are typically due Jan-Feb). Public universities also face the ADA Title II web-accessibility deadline of **April 26, 2027** and will favor vendors with a conformance report. [Data]
- **Bonuses (each kills an objection):**
  1. *Research-landscape report for the provost* (kills "leadership won't care")
  2. *Side-by-side pilot design with your posting tool* (kills "we already pay for ForagerOne")
  3. *Co-authored CUR/NCUR presentation of your outcomes* (kills "what's in it for my office?")
  4. *Pre-filled HECVAT 4 + accessibility conformance report* (kills "IT will block it")
- **Guarantee (conditional):** *"Your atlas goes live within 45 days of receiving your directory export, covering at least 95% of in-scope investigators with at least one evidence item. If it doesn't, you pay nothing until it does. And if fewer than half your advisors use it in the first semester, cancel at semester's end and we refund the unused months."* [Founder to confirm risk appetite]

## Step 6: Commodity Check
- **Can a prospect compare this side-by-side with ForagerOne?** Only if it's sold as a "research opportunity platform". Sold as **a lab discovery layer with a coverage guarantee, a landscape report and outcomes reporting that links to ForagerOne**, there is no like-for-like comparison. **Category of one, as long as positioning discipline holds.**

## Red Flags
- **Perceived Likelihood 5/10.** Without Emory outcomes and enterprise documents, the offer reads as a student project. Fix before selling outside Emory.
